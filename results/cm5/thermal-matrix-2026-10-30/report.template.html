Takeaways

  • With no airflow, a CM5 running DEXI-OS heats past the soft-throttle point in about 14 minutes. Adding YOLO puts it at 89–91 °C and keeps it throttled, with the ARM clock dipping to 1000 MHz.
  • Any fan fixes it: DEXI-OS settles in the low 50s °C, and DEXI-OS + YOLO around 60 °C, with no throttling. In flight, prop wash does the same job (see the 2026-06-30 takeoff profile).
  • CPU load during the DEXI-OS run was flat, near 20%, while the temperature climbed, so the throttling comes from heat buildup with no airflow, not from extra work.
  • With YOLO and no fan, CPU % rose from 34% to 47% as the clock dropped: the same work takes a larger share of a slower CPU.
